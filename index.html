How to locate your 64-character Transaction Hash / TxID:
• MetaMask / Coinbase Wallet: Open the app → tap the sent transaction → tap "View on Explorer" (or "Transaction Details") → copy the Transaction Hash (starts with 0x...).
• Trust Wallet: Tap the transfer in your history → tap "More Details" → copy the Hash.
• Exchanges (Binance, OKX, Coinbase, Kraken): Go to Wallet → Spot → Withdrawal History → tap the transfer → copy the TxID.
