What is "SLA Overdue"?

          
Web3 Response Speed & Critical 24-Hour Window

        

      

      
    

    

      

        SLA stands for Service Level Agreement — the emergency turnaround standard for cryptocurrency theft investigations.
      

      

        The Golden 24-Hour Window:

        Perpetrators typically transfer drained cryptocurrency across cross-chain bridges and deposit into centralized exchanges (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase) within 6 to 24 hours to liquidate into fiat currency.
      

      

        Any intake case that remains pending or unserviced for longer than 24 hours is automatically flagged as SLA Overdue so senior supervisors can expedite subpoena generation before assets leave the exchange.
      

      

        Once a case is moved to CEX Hold, Restituted, or Closed/Archived, the SLA overdue alert is cleared.
