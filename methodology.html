The Five Pillars of Defensible Evidence

In courtrooms, regulatory hearings, and law enforcement bureaus, evidence must satisfy the Daubert Standard of scientific reliability. NexusShield's investigative methodology produces verifiable digital chain-of-custody documentation that prosecutors and exchanges accept.

STAGE 01 • ASSESSMENT

Initial Case Assessment & Ingress

We evaluate the incident structure: fake brokers, wallet drainer smart contracts, WhatsApp/Telegram impersonation, or unauthorized access. We establish the initial time of loss and extract raw transaction hashes directly from blockchain mempools.

STAGE 02 • TRACING

Multi-Hop Blockchain Mapping

Scammers split funds through "peel chains" and cross-chain liquidity bridges (Arbitrum, Polygon, Avalanche). Our forensic clustering algorithms map the fragmented path, isolating the consolidated addresses where the scammer holds the stolen assets.

STAGE 03 • COORDINATION

VASP & Law Enforcement Liaison

A blockchain wallet address has no value without a real-world legal hook. We pinpoint the exact destination Centralized Exchange (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase) and prepare formal freeze requests under KYC and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) treaties.

STAGE 04 • INTERCEPTION

Emergency Exchange Freezes

We file administrative hold packets with exchange financial crime desks. The scammer's destination account is frozen before they can liquidate funds into fiat currency or transfer off-platform.

STAGE 05 • RESTITUTION

Restitution Support & Recovery

We assist your legal counsel or law enforcement agency with court orders, subpoenas, and Mutual Legal Assistance Treaties (MLAT) to release the frozen funds back into your verified, clean restitution wallet.

The 4-Vector Threat Exposure Index (0–100)

Global Ledger aligned risk assessment methodology evaluating illicit contamination.
1. Entity Counterparty Risk

Evaluates the regulatory status of the holding platform (unregulated off-ramp, scam broker, or tier-1 CEX).

2. Source of Funds Risk

Identifies whether assets originated from wallet drainer contracts, phishing approvals, or unauthorized private key sweeps.

3. Use of Funds Risk

Tracks multi-hop peel chains, micro-splits, bridge crossings, and mixer attempts (Tornado Cash, Garantex).

4. Address Taint Risk

Cross-referenced against OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) and EU Financial Sanctions registries.
